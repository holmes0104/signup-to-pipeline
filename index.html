From sign‑up to pipeline

How one person moves through a weather API company's marketing stack, and how I check a campaign before it goes out. Made for my application to the Marketing Automation and Technology Specialist role at Vaisala Xweather.

  • 1 · Lifecycle replay. Pick a person. See the event data, the Customer.io automation, the Salesforce record and the attribution change at each step.
  • 2 · Preflight checker. A pre-send QA tool that runs in your browser. It opens on a campaign with planted errors.

Fictional people, companies and numbers. Not connected to, or endorsed by, Vaisala or Xweather. The free tier (15,000 accesses a month) and the €300 subscription come from xweather.com's public pricing page, checked 21 November 2026.
